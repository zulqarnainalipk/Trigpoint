      Review workflow
      
      
        
        
      1. Press N to open the next unreviewed site. The map flies to it and frames the box.
        2. 
        
      2. Check the satellite image. Drag the pin to centre the box on the facility and drag a corner square, or press - and +, to set its size. Press E for the quick edit strip if you only need the essentials.
        3. 
        
      3. Open the sections of the details bar on the right: screening flags, neighbours within 150 km and quality flags. Fold either bar with [ and ], or press F for a clean map.
        4. 
        
      4. Press V to keep (saves your edits, ticks verified, opens the next site), X to reject, or S to skip for later.
        5. 
        
      5. Open Export, choose "Kept sites", and download the cleaned file.
        6. 
      
      
      
      Reject is reversible: a rejected site stays in the file with a rejected flag, is left out of "kept" exports, and comes back with U or Undo. Delete removes the site for good after a confirmation.
      
      
      Shortcuts
      
      
      Shortcuts work when no text field is focused. Ctrl and Cmd shortcuts work everywhere.
      
      
      
        
          
      J / KNext / previous site in the list
      
          
      NNext unreviewed site (shift+N: previous)
      
          
      VKeep: save, verify, go to next unreviewed
      
          
      XReject and go to next unreviewed
      
          
      SSkip for later
      
          
      UMark as not reviewed again
      
          
      Ctrl+SSave the current site (or create, when adding)
      
          
      Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+ZUndo / redo, including keep and reject
      
          
      Arrow keysNudge the box (Shift: bigger step)
      
          
      [ / ]Fold or open the site list (left) / the details bar (right)
      
          
      FFocus mode: hide both bars and extra controls; press again to restore
      
          
      EQuick edit strip: essentials only (Enter saves, Esc closes)
      
          
      - / +Smaller / larger box (next preset size)
      
          
      TApply the typical box size for this facility type
      
          
      ZZoom the map to fit the box
      
          
      GFocus the "paste lat, lon" box (pasting coordinates anywhere also works)
      
          
      /Focus the search box
      
          
      L B R P O HToggle layers: label window, background ring, split radius, pilot sites, other candidates, exact pixel grid (H)
      
          
      MSwitch basemap
      
          
      In the site listUp and down arrows, Home and End move through the list, Enter opens a site, Shift+Space ticks or unticks it for bulk actions
      
          
      EscClose a panel, cancel add, or deselect
      
          
      ?Open this help
      
              
      
      
      
      Reading the map
      
      
        
        
      • The blue box is the chip box you are fixing (1 to 8 km). The dashed box around it is the fixed label window that the satellite targets use. The two dashed rings are the 30 and 75 km background annulus.
        • 
        
      • The dotted circle is the split radius. Any candidate or pilot site inside it shares a train or test split with the selected site.
        • 
        
      • Kept sites are circles, pending sites are diamonds, rejected sites are crosses and pilot sites are triangles. Colour is never the only cue.
